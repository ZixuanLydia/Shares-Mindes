For local browser loading, vision.js wraps the upstream CommonJS SDK and Wasm
loader in a factory that can run in a Worker, and omits the SDK source map link.
The SDK and loader logic are unmodified. The Wasm binary and model bytes are
unchanged after gzip decompression and base64 decoding.
